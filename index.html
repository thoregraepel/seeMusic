About seeMusic

seeMusic was created by Thore Graepel and Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) to make music visible. Each note becomes a spatial grating whose frequency matches its pitch — finer stripes for high notes, coarser for low. Superimposed gratings create interference patterns just as light waves do.

MIDI mode

Select a MIDI file from the dropdown or load your own.

Audio mode

Load any MP3, WAV, or OGG file with Load Audio. The app analyses the real-time FFT of the audio and maps all 88 piano notes (A0–C8) to gratings, weighted by their loudness. Use the FFT Threshold slider to filter out background noise.

Live mode

Connect a MIDI keyboard via USB and click Live to visualise what you play in real time.

Microphone mode

Click 🎤 Mic to visualise live audio from your microphone.

Visual controls

  • In colour mode each pitch class maps to a hue (C = red, +30° per semitone).
  • Hyperbolic mode warps the geometry with a tanh mapping (Poincaré-disk-like).
  • Waveform changes the grating profile: sine, square, triangle, or sawtooth.
  • Superposition controls how gratings combine: sum, product, or max.
  • Spectral tilt weights amplitudes by pitch, emphasising highs or lows.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space Play / Pause  ·  A Toggle audio  ·  V Toggle visual  ·  F Fullscreen

⚠ Photosensitivity warning: seeMusic produces rapidly changing visual patterns that may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy or similar conditions. Use with caution or turn off the visual display if sensitive.
